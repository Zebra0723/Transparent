      04About

      Nothing to hide.

      Transparent celebrates hardware that shows how it works: clear shells, smoked acrylic, resin-cast cases, exposed flex cables, and LEDs that blink behind a window instead of a wall.

      Everything you add here (mods, photos, ideas, likes and comments) is stored locally in your browser and never uploaded. Export a backup to keep it safe or move it to another device.

      Photographing a clear mod

      1. Shoot on dark. A black backdrop makes the internals stand out through the shell.
      2. Light from behind. Back- or under-lighting shows off traces and components.
      3. Show the guts. Include at least one photo mid-build with the board out.
