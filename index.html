      About

      Nothing to hide.

      Transparent celebrates hardware that shows its work. Clear shells, smoked acrylic, resin-cast cases, exposed flex cables and every LED that blinks behind a window instead of a wall.

      Everything here — mods, photos, ideas, likes and comments — is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Export a backup to keep it safe or move it to another device.

      Tips for great photos

      • Shoot against a dark background so the internals pop through the shell.
      • Backlight from behind or below to show off traces and components.
      • Include one shot mid-build with the board out.
